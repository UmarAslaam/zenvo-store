About Zenvo

A small team making everyday tech easy to buy.

Our story

Zenvo started with a simple frustration: too many gadgets, too little honesty. We began as two friends testing headphones on their commute and sharing the ones worth buying.

Today we pick a small range of gear we would use ourselves, check it for quality and sell it at fair prices.
